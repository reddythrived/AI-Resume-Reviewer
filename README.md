## Overview

AI Resume Reviewer is an AI-powered web application designed to analyze resumes and provide intelligent feedback for improving resume quality and ATS compatibility.

The platform helps students, freshers, and professionals optimize their resumes using AI-generated suggestions, skill analysis, and keyword evaluation.

## Working Process

### Step 1: Resume Upload

Users upload their resume in PDF format through the web interface.

### Step 2: Resume Parsing

The system extracts text and important information such as skills, education, projects, and experience from the uploaded resume.

### Step 3: AI Analysis

AI analyzes the resume content to evaluate ATS compatibility, keyword usage, technical skills, and overall resume quality.

### Step 4: Score Generation

The platform generates an ATS score and identifies strengths, weaknesses, and missing keywords.

### Step 5: Feedback & Suggestions

Users receive personalized suggestions and improvement tips to make their resumes more professional and job-ready.
